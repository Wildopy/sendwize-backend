Privacy

Who we are

Brightside Home Ltd (company number 12345678), 14 Mill Lane, Leeds LS1 4AB, is the data controller for your personal data. Contact us at privacy@brightsidehome.co.uk.

How we use your information

We use your details to process and deliver orders (lawful basis: contract), to send you marketing emails where you have given consent (lawful basis: consent), and to prevent fraud (lawful basis: legitimate interests).

Who we share it with

We share personal data with processors who help us run the shop: Shopify (orders), Klaviyo (email) and Royal Mail (delivery). We never sell your data to third parties.

International transfers

Some processors store data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA).

How long we keep it

We retain order records for six years for tax purposes. We keep marketing consent records for as long as you stay subscribed and for two years after you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You have the right of access to your data, the right to rectification, the right to erasure and the right to object to marketing at any time.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.

Cookies

We only use cookies that are strictly necessary to run the shop, such as the basket cookie. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. If we add any cookies that are not essential, we will ask for your consent first through a cookie banner.
