Your dashboard
One place for the score,
the £ and the fixes.
First you answer a few questions about your business (sector, turnover, list size and so on), so every £ figure is sized to you. Then the dashboard shows your score, what poor practice has already cost you, your open regulatory exposure and an action list from every tool.
🧾 Why this number?
Every action item shows the evidence behind it and the real enforcement cases it is based on, including the closest match to your business.
🎚️ What-if mode
Tick off fixes to see how far your exposure would drop before you do the work. Mark fixes done, or dismiss ones that don't apply.

Group 1 of 4 — Before you send
Check a campaign
before it goes out.
Catch problems while they are still cheap to fix: before a single contact sees the email, ad or post.
🚦 Send-day verdict
Checks the copy, the landing pages and the send list together and gives one answer: Go, Fix first or Hold.
You get: a verdict saved as a dated record that you checked
🔎 Copy checker
Paste any email, SMS, ad or social post. It flags fake urgency, unproven claims, hidden fees, missing ad labels and missing unsubscribe details, with a rewrite for each.
You get: findings and rewrites that become fixes on your dashboard

Group 2 of 4 — Your list
Know who you can
legally email.
Upload an export from any email platform (Klaviyo, Mailchimp and the rest all work).
🩺 List health
Scores every contact for consent, deliverability and value. Flags old consent, risky sources, soft opt-in misuse and people who unsubscribed from another list.
You get: a green, amber and red split, a list value and a re-consent email draft
📈 Audience read
Upload your send history. It compares unsubscribes and complaints with UK benchmarks and shows what each send did to your list.
You get: a 12-month list forecast, your next-send call and a Black Friday plan

Group 3 of 4 — Website and suppliers
Check where your data
is collected and shared.
Most of your risk sits outside the email itself: your website, and the companies you share data with.
🍪 Site sweep
Verify your domain and it scans the whole site for cookies set before consent, the cookie banner, your privacy notice and sign-up forms.
You get: each problem turned into a fix
🤝 Third parties
One register for suppliers, data partners, affiliates and influencers, with a risk score for each. Reads a supplier’s privacy terms for you.
You get: supplier and partner gaps on your dashboard

Group 4 of 4 — Records and practice
Be ready if a
regulator asks.
If the ICO, ASA or CMA get in touch, what you can prove matters as much as what you did.
🛡️ Campaign defence
Builds an evidence file for a campaign: copy findings, a scan of every page it links to, approval with your name and time, then watches those pages for changes after launch.
You get: a frozen, approved record for each campaign
⚖️ Regulator simulator
Pick the ICO, ASA or CMA and rehearse an investigation using your real profile and findings, from first-contact letter to penalty estimate.
You get: a simulated investigation you can reopen from history
