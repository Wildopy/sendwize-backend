Ads and pricing work differently

Ad claims (ASA)

The ASA doesn't fine. An upheld ruling is published under your name for good, and serious cases go to Trading Standards. So we show the ruling and how likely a referral is, never a made-up fine.

Prices and urgency (CMA)

The CMA can now fine directly, but there aren't enough decisions yet to give a realistic range. So we show the legal maximum for a business your size, and nothing more.

What these figures are not

Not a prediction. A real outcome depends on things no software sees, like how you co-operate and how fast you put things right.

Not legal advice. For your own position, speak to a solicitor who specialises in UK data protection or consumer law.

Every factor and its weight

Factors come from three places: your profile; rules about the finding itself (contacts affected, data types, supplier agreements, how long it's been open); and, only if you ask, an AI reading. The AI can only tick factors from its part of this list, and only by quoting your evidence word for word. Anything else is thrown away.
